Step 1 — Choose the Child
Only children with a registered birth date can have a schedule generated.
How it works. Each vaccine dose falls due a fixed number of months after birth. Generating reads the child's date of birth, works out every due date, and queues the doses that are not already on file. Doses already past their date are filed as Overdue so they surface on the filter. Running it twice adds nothing.
